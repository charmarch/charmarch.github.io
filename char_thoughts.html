songwriting, to me

may 21, 2026

2024 was a strange year for me. From April to September -- the best six months of the year in Canada, mind you -- I partook in a romance, which to the other participant was probably more like a fling, or realistically, a bout of pollen. I used the words entanglement or situationship. And it was crazy, the number of conversations I had with friends about him, what I possibly wanted, or what he might be thinking or feeling. I watched at least five cumulative hours of Instagram reels dating advice about avoidant types.

It is fun to be in the throes of endearment. It is even more fun when there's a bit of doubt about the reciprocity, because the hints of it become even sweeter. You're sleuthing and feeling, and the stakes are high because it's your life, but low because it's not something that really matters, and it is addictive to wonder and swoon and be hurt and feel better. (Annie Ernaux's Getting Lost was the perfect picture of the emotional heights and depths of this sort of relationship.) And I knew this push and pull on paper, but being in it was different. This entanglement really did wrap me up and occupy my mind and affect my priorities and take my time and for all the conversations I had dissecting the possibilities and emotions, it was in song that I was honest about what it was: hope for something impossible, that superficially had to do with him.
